Distance assumptions

Students needed to keep a school open. One stream across Forms 1–4 at 40 per class is 160. Distance never calls for more schools than the ward’s young people could fill.

Capacity assumptions

About 360 is a two-stream school (Forms 1–4, 45 per class); 720 is four streams.

Enrollment assumptions

Smooths out students who cross ward lines to attend school.
